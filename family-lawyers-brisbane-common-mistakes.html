What to Ask Before Sending Documents

While the initial enquiry process is generally free and non-obligatory, it represents only the first step towards potential legal representation. Before submitting any sensitive documents, it is advisable to ask key questions. Confirm whether the solicitor specialises in the specific area of family law relevant to your situation. Inquire about who will be handling your matter and what fee structure applies before substantive work commences. Keeping the initial exchange brief allows the solicitor to efficiently classify your enquiry.

A practical initial message should include the matter category, the state, the urgency, and your desired outcome. Detailed personal information, allegations, financial records, and other private documents should be withheld until the solicitor outlines the formal intake process. Essential questions to ask include:

  • Can the practitioner assist with this specific type of family law issue?
  • Is a conflict check required before your details can be reviewed?
  • What fee information can be provided before work begins?
  • Which documents should be sent first, and what is the preferred method of transmission?
